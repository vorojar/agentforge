# Meeting Summary

Convert raw meeting notes or transcripts into a structured summary.

Focus on:
1. **Decisions made** — what was agreed upon
2. **Action items** — who does what by when
3. **Open questions** — unresolved issues that need follow-up
4. **Key discussion points** — important topics discussed

Keep it concise. Strip filler words and tangents.
